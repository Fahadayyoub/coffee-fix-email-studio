## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** Your Steep Club seat is ready
- **Summary:** Over four weeks of emails, we invite our waitlist to join The Steep Club, with a different angle for beginners, gift buyers and daily tea drinkers.
- **Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a subscription between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Other goals:**
  - Learn which of the three groups joins most, to shape future emails.
  - Collect 3 to 5 real quotes from early members, with their permission, for later campaigns.
  - Keep unsubscribes under 1% per email.

**A note on the goal.** 300 out of 2,000 is 15%. That's high for email, even to a warm waitlist. It's reachable only if the offer is clear and the subscription page is easy. Section 7 sets weekly checkpoints so we know early if we're behind.

## 3. Who we're writing to

About 2,000 people who asked to hear about The Steep Club. They already know us, so this is a decision-stage campaign. Our job is to make joining feel easy and worth it, not to explain who Coffee Fix is.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might stop them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** | Like the idea of tea, but don't know where to start | A simple way in, no pressure, help choosing | "I won't know what to do with it." "What if I don't like it?" |
| **Gift buyers** | Shopping for someone else, likely for Christmas | A gift that feels thoughtful and arrives on time | Delivery dates. Not knowing the other person's taste. |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | Already drink tea most days | Good tea without the hassle of buying it | "Is it better than what I buy now?" Price. Being locked in. |

**If we don't know who is in which group:** send everyone the launch email with three links, "I'm new to tea", "I'm buying a gift" and "I drink tea every day". Each click sorts that person into a group for later emails. Anyone who doesn't click gets the general version.

## 4. Key messages

**Core message:** The Steep Club brings fresh, real tea to your door every month, picked with care by the people who make your coffee.

| Message | For | Proof we need |
|---|---|---|
| You don't need to know anything about tea to start. Each box tells you how to make it. | Beginners | `[TBC]` brewing card or guide in each box |
| A gift that keeps arriving, month after month. | Gift buyers | `[TBC]` gift option, gift message, order-by date |
| Your daily cup, sorted. No more running out. | Daily drinkers | `[TBC]` what's in each box, how often |
| Join or stop when you like. | Everyone | `[TBC]` cancel policy. Only say this if it's true. |
| Waitlist members come first. | Everyone | `[TBC]` early access or welcome offer |

**Proof rule.** Only say "100% authentic" about something we can show, like where a tea is grown. Give the real detail instead: "picked in `[region]`, packed in our shop on `[day]`".

**Brand rules for this campaign** (from `brand/voice.md`):

- **No health claims, either way.** This matters most with tea and matcha. Don't use "detox", "healthy", "calming for your body" or "full of antioxidants".
- **No "best" claims.** Don't say "the best tea in town" or "better than supermarket tea".
- **No unproven labels.** Don't say "organic", "fair trade" or "eco-friendly" unless we can prove it.
- **No allergen promises.** Point people to staff.
- **No pushy words.** Don't say "hurry", "last chance" or "don't miss out", and don't use all caps or more than one exclamation mark. To give a deadline, state the date calmly: "Waitlist pricing ends on Sunday 3 January."
- **One emoji per line at most.** 🍵 suits this campaign.
- **Every email ends with:**
  > Your coffee is waiting for your dull day. Just fix it.
  > — Coffee Fix

## 8. How we'll measure it

**Main measure:** new subscriptions from the waitlist. Target: 300 by Sunday 3 January.

**Weekly checkpoints:**

| By end of | Total members | If we're behind |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 (13 Dec) | 120 | Check the subscription page first, then E1's click rate |
| Week 2 (20 Dec) | 200 | Lean harder on gift buyers before Christmas |
| Week 3 (27 Dec) | 250 | Keep week 4 as planned. Don't add extra sends. |
| Week 4 (3 Jan) | 300 | |

**Other measures:**

| Measure | What it tells us | How |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate per email | Whether subject lines work | Email tool |
| Click rate per email | Whether the message works | Email tool |
| Joins per group | Which group responds most | A tracked link for each group and email |
| Unsubscribe rate | Whether we're sending too much | Email tool. Pause if over 1% on any send. |
| Gift subscriptions | Whether the Christmas angle worked | Subscription page |

**Reporting:** a short note in `notes/` every Monday during the campaign, and a wrap-up by 8 January.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **15% is a high target.** | Watch the week 1 checkpoint. If we're well under 120, fix the page and offer before sending more emails. |
| **Christmas inboxes are full, and people are away.** | Send the strongest emails in weeks 1 and 2. Stay quiet on 24 to 26 December. Keep subject lines short and calm. |
| **Gifts arrive late.** | Confirm the real order-by date before writing E4a. If we can't promise 25 December, say so and offer a gift card or a printable gift note instead `[TBC]`. |
| **Tea makes health claims tempting.** | Every draft goes through `brand-review` before it's final. Health words are a hard stop. |
| **We don't know who's in which group.** | Use the three links in E1 to sort people. |
