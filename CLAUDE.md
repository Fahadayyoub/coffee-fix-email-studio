# Email Studio

This studio is where we plan and write email campaigns for a small business. It holds what we know about the brand, who we're writing to, the campaigns themselves, and any notes along the way.

## Folders

- **brand/** – The business's voice, style, logos, and key facts that every email should follow.
- **leads/** – Lists and descriptions of the people and groups we want to reach.
- **campaigns/** – One folder or file per email campaign: the plan, the drafts, and the final copy.
- **notes/** – Ideas, meeting notes, research, and anything else that doesn't fit elsewhere.

## Rules

- **Write in plain English.** Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon.
- **Never send email to real people.** Write drafts and plans only. Do not send, schedule, or queue any email to anyone else, even as a test.
  - The one exception: through Resend, you may send to Resend's test addresses (any address ending in `@resend.dev`) and to my own address, muhammadfahadayyoub@gmail.com. Nothing else.
  - A guard will enforce this in the next step. Until it is in place, follow this rule yourself.
- **Ask me before deleting anything.** This includes files, folders, drafts, and lead lists.
