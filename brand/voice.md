# Coffee Fix brand voice

Coffee Fix is a premium coffee shop. We sell coffee, matcha, coffee beans, sandwiches and burgers. We write for everyone, from Gen Z to older customers, so every email should be easy to read and feel welcoming at any age.

Tagline: **Fix your day with Coffee Fix**

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Warm.** We sound like a friendly neighbour saying hello, not a company selling something.
- **Friendly.** Short sentences, everyday words. Talk to one person ("you"), not a crowd.
- **Premium.** Polished and calm. We never shout, beg or sound like a sale flyer.

## 2. Words we use

- fresh, original, real, authentic
- 100% authentic beans and ingredients
- clean, cared for, made with care
- your fix, fix your day, your coffee is waiting
- welcome, see you soon, come in, sit down, take a moment

Keep it simple. Light emoji (like ☕) is fine now and then. Never use more than one in a line.

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Anything that sounds cheap:** cheap, budget, bargain, dirt cheap, steal, low price, clearance.
- **Pushy sales words:** hurry, last chance, don't miss out, act now, LIMITED!!!, all caps, lots of exclamation marks.
- **Health words:** healthy, unhealthy, guilt-free, superfood, detox, low fat, burns fat, boosts immunity.
- **Heavy slang or jargon** that one age group won't understand.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No health claims, either way.** Never say a drink or food item is healthy, unhealthy, or good for your body. That includes matcha.
- **No "best" claims.** Never say "best coffee in town", "number 1" or "the best", or claim to be better than other shops.
- **No labels we can't prove.** Never say "organic", "fair trade", "carbon neutral" or "eco-friendly" unless we have proof we can show.
- **No allergen promises.** Never say something is allergen-free, nut-free, gluten-free or safe for allergies. Point people to staff instead.
- **Keep "100% authentic" and "clean environment" true and specific.** Only say it about things we can back up, like our beans or how we keep the shop. When you can, give a real detail instead of a vague word.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email ends with:

> Your coffee is waiting for your dull day. Just fix it.
> — Coffee Fix
