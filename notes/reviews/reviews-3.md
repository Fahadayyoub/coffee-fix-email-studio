# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (set 3 of 6)

Practice data. These reviews are made up. None of them came from real customers.

---

### 41. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Joanna V.
The oolong is the one that hooked me. Creamy and floral. Wish the box told me which teas could be steeped more than once.

### 42. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Patrick E.
Waited ten days, then it came with a torn outer box. Two pouches had leaked into each other. Sencha with bits of hibiscus in it.

### 43. ★★★★★ (5/5) Mina J.
Every single tea was good, which never happens with samplers. The Darjeeling and the Hojicha are now on my shopping list.

### 44. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Stuart H.
The masala chai was great. The rest were fine. The box looks lovely on the shelf, which my wife appreciated.

### 45. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) Wendy G.
Charged me twice, sent it once, and it took over two weeks. I'm sure the tea is nice but I've lost patience.

### 46. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Arjun R.
Proper loose leaf, not dust. The Assam is strong enough to stand a spoon in. Only a small amount of each, so it goes fast.

### 47. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Eva K.
The brewing card lists times for "green", "black" and "other". Which one is the Genmaicha? Which one is the White Peony? I had to guess.

### 48. ★★★★★ (5/5) Kwame A.
Gave this to my mum for her birthday. She loved the jasmine pearls so much she called me just to talk about them.

### 49. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Rebecca L.
Not enough of the good stuff. One pouch of Tie Guan Yin, three pouches of fruit tea. I'd swap the balance around.

### 50. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Dmitri P.
Lapsang souchong was smoky and bold, my favourite by far. Packaging is nice but the plastic tray feels unnecessary.

### 51. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Shona M.
Chamomile was lovely, peppermint was lovely, the black teas were just okay. Took about a week to arrive.

### 52. ★★★★★ (5/5) Farah B.
I didn't expect to love green tea, but the Dragonwell changed my mind. Nutty and smooth. The tasting notes on each pouch helped.

### 53. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Colin D.
The box rattled when it arrived. Loose leaves everywhere because one pouch wasn't sealed properly. Spent ages tidying up.

### 54. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Isla W.
Lovely gift box. The Earl Grey and the Ceylon are both very good. I'd like a bigger pouch of the Earl Grey.

### 55. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Hamza U.
Decent but shipping was slow, nine working days. The vanilla rooibos was nice, sweet without sugar.

### 56. ★★★★★ (5/5) Naomi S.
Opened it and the whole kitchen smelled of tea. The White Peony was delicate and lovely. Worth every penny.

### 57. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Phil R.
The QR code on the brewing card goes to a page that doesn't load. So I had no idea how long to brew anything.

### 58. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Saira T.
The Sencha was fresh and bright. I'd give it five but there's only enough for two pots and I want more now.

### 59. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) George N.
Good teas, average experience. Delivery took a week and the box was squashed. The Assam made up for it a bit.

### 60. ★★★★★ (5/5) Lina O.
The Hojicha and the Genmaicha both made me very happy. Clear labels, nice smell, fast delivery. No complaints at all.
