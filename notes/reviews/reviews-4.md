# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (set 4 of 6)

Practice data. These reviews are made up. None of them came from real customers.

---

### 61. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Adam C.
The jasmine pearls were the best thing in the box, and there were only about ten of them. Felt like a tease.

### 62. ★★★★★ (5/5) Mariam F.
Arrived in two days, perfectly packed. The Darjeeling first flush is something special. Will be back for more.

### 63. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Trevor B.
My box had two pouches of peppermint and no Lapsang. Asked for the missing one, still waiting.

### 64. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Kirsty L.
Really enjoyed the oolong and the Hojicha. The brewing card was a bit hard to read, tiny grey text on a grey card.

### 65. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Usman H.
The Assam and Ceylon are nice but taste quite similar to me. I'd swap one of them for something more unusual.

### 66. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) Diane P.
Took sixteen days to arrive. Tracking said "delivered" a week before it actually turned up. Not a good start.

### 67. ★★★★★ (5/5) Theo M.
The masala chai is better than the chai at my local café. Rich and spicy. I've already bought a big bag.

### 68. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Aisha D.
Lovely selection. The Sencha was fresh and the Genmaicha was cosy. Packaging is pretty but a bit wasteful.

### 69. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Robert K.
The brewing instructions said 5 minutes for the Dragonwell. It came out so bitter I poured it away. Later read it should be 2.

### 70. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Nina G.
Nice teas, but the box was crushed and the lid won't close anymore. Not great for a gift.

### 71. ★★★★★ (5/5) Bilal S.
I'm a coffee person and wasn't sure about this. The Lapsang souchong won me over. Smoky, bold, a bit like a good espresso.

### 72. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Harriet Y.
The White Peony and the chamomile are my evening teas now. Would love a bigger pouch of the White Peony.

### 73. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Jamal W.
Fine. Some winners, like the Tie Guan Yin. Some I didn't finish, like the vanilla rooibos. Shipping was slow but okay.

### 74. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Linda E.
Paid for express shipping and it still took eight days. The teas were alright but I wanted it for a birthday and it was late.

### 75. ★★★★★ (5/5) Oscar J.
Fantastic. Each pouch had a little card with the tea's story. The Earl Grey was fragrant and not too perfumey.

### 76. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Priya N.
The Darjeeling and the Assam were both great. A few leaves had escaped their pouches, but nothing was ruined.

### 77. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Callum F.
I like the idea, but the brewing card doesn't match the pouch labels. The card says "Green Tea No. 2" and the pouches just say "Sencha".

### 78. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) Sandra H.
The box smelled of smoke when I opened it and every tea tasted of Lapsang. Couldn't enjoy any of the delicate ones.

### 79. ★★★★★ (5/5) Yusuf A.
Perfect intro to loose leaf tea. The Hojicha is roasty and calm. Delivery was quick and the box was spotless.

### 80. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Megan R.
The jasmine pearls were lovely. My only complaint is that I want a full tin of them and the site was sold out.
