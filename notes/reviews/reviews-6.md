# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (set 6 of 6)

Practice data. These reviews are made up. None of them came from real customers.

### 101. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Hugo M.
The black teas were good, the herbal ones were forgettable. Shipping took about a week, which is slow for a small box.

### 102. ★★★★★ (5/5) Asma Y.
The White Peony is so gentle and sweet. I've told everyone at work about it. The box itself is gorgeous too.

### 104. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Nicole F.

### 105. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) Dennis O.

### 106. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Rukhsana A.

### 107. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Peter L.

### 108. ★★★★★ (5/5) Jasmine K.

### 109. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Andrew T.

### 110. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Layla R.

### 112. ★★★★★ (5/5) Noor F.

### 114. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Omar K.

### 116. ★★★★★ (5/5) Arif D.

### 118. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Hassan G.

### 119. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Ellie N.

### 120. ★★★★★ (5/5) Sami Z.
Best gift I've had in ages. Fresh teas, clear labels, beautiful box. The Genmaicha and the jasmine pearls were my favourites.
