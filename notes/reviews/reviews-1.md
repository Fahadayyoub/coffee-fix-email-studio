# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (set 1 of 6)

Practice data. These reviews are made up. None of them came from real customers.

---

### 1. ★★★★★ (5/5) Priya S.
The Darjeeling first flush was the star for me. Light, floral, and nothing like the tea bags I grew up with. Already ordered a full tin.

### 2. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Mark D.
Nice range of teas, but the box arrived squashed on one corner and two of the little pouches had split. Jasmine pearls everywhere.

### 3. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Helen W.
Took eleven days to arrive. By the time it came I'd almost forgotten I ordered it. The Assam breakfast was decent though.

### 4. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Tariq M.
Loved the Genmaicha, that toasty rice smell is great. Only one small pouch of it though, which I finished in two days. Wish there was more.

### 5. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Jo B.
The brewing card says "steep 2–5 minutes at 70–100°C". That isn't instructions, that's a shrug. I had to look each tea up online.

### 6. ★★★★★ (5/5) Grace L.
Bought this as a present for my dad and he's been working through it one tea a day. The Earl Grey with real bergamot was his favourite.

### 7. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) Kevin R.
Never got the full box. Mine was missing the oolong and the rooibos entirely, and customer service took a week to answer.

### 8. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Anika P.
The Sencha was fresh and grassy, exactly how I like it. Packaging looks lovely too. Knocked off a star because the pouches don't reseal.

### 9. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Sam O.
Lapsang souchong tastes like a campfire. Some people will love it. I did not. The rest of the box was nice enough.

### 10. ★★★★★ (5/5) Elena V.
Every tea was labelled clearly and smelled amazing when I opened the box. The White Peony was gentle and sweet. Really happy with it.

### 11. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Chris T.
Ruined my first cup of Dragonwell because the card didn't say green tea needs cooler water. Bitter as anything. Second try was better.

### 12. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Nadia H.
Great way to find out what you like. Turns out I'm a Tie Guan Yin person. Only enough for about three pots, which felt stingy.

### 13. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Ben A.
Teas were good, but shipping was slow and there was no tracking update for five days. I kept checking for nothing.

### 14. ★★★★★ (5/5) Ruth K.
The masala chai blend was spot on, proper cardamom and ginger. Made it with oat milk and it was perfect on a cold evening.

### 15. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Leo F.
So much plastic. Each tea in its own sealed sachet, inside a plastic tray, inside a box, inside a padded bag. Felt a bit much.

### 16. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Yasmin E.
The Hojicha was a lovely surprise. Roasty and smooth, nothing like the green teas I expected. Would buy a bigger bag of that alone.

### 17. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Owen G.
Fine, but the "vanilla rooibos" barely tasted of vanilla. The chamomile was nice for bedtime. Middle of the road overall.

### 18. ★★★★★ (5/5) Clara N.
Arrived in three days, neatly packed, with a handwritten thank-you note. The jasmine pearls unfurling in the pot was a little treat.

### 19. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Marcus J.
Twelve teas sounds like a lot until you see the pouches are tiny. Barely two cups each. For the price I expected more.

### 20. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Fiona C.
The Ceylon black tea is my new morning cup. Brewing guide was a bit confusing, the times and temperatures were on a separate card from the tea names.
