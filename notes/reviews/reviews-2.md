# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (set 2 of 6)

Practice data. These reviews are made up. None of them came from real customers.

---

### 21. ★★★★★ (5/5) Imran Q.
I've tried a lot of samplers and this one had the freshest leaves. The Assam was malty and strong, just what I need before work.

### 22. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Lucy H.
Pretty box, okay teas. The peppermint was lovely but the Earl Grey tasted a bit stale, like it had been sitting a while.

### 23. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) Derek S.
Box arrived wet and every pouch had soaked through. Couldn't drink any of it. Got a refund in the end but it took three emails.

### 24. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Hana Y.
The Sencha and the Genmaicha were both excellent. I'd give five stars if the card told me how much leaf to use per cup.

### 25. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Paul M.
Shipping took nearly two weeks. Teas were good once they got here. The oolong was the highlight.

### 26. ★★★★★ (5/5) Bea R.
Bought one for me and one for my sister. We text each other about which tea we're on. The White Peony is our shared favourite.

### 27. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Victor L.
The brewing instructions use symbols I couldn't work out. Is that a leaf or a spoon? Is that three minutes or three cups? Confusing.

### 28. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Amira K.
The Tie Guan Yin was gorgeous, I re-steeped it four times. Just wish there was more than one tiny pouch of it.

### 29. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Neil B.
Good teas, but half the box is herbal. I wanted more real tea and fewer fruit blends.

### 30. ★★★★★ (5/5) Sophie D.
Packaging is beautiful, like opening a little gift. The Darjeeling smelled like peaches. I didn't know tea could do that.

### 31. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Gareth P.
The Lapsang and the masala chai were packed next to each other and now everything in the box smells smoky. Bad packing.

### 32. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Leila A.
Lovely variety. The Hojicha is the one I keep reaching for. Arrived a few days later than promised, but nothing major.

### 33. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Martin O.
Dragonwell was nice. The jasmine pearls were nice. Nothing blew me away. Fine if you're new to loose tea.

### 34. ★★★★★ (5/5) Zoe F.
I'm a total beginner and this was a fun way to learn. The chamomile and the vanilla rooibos were my evening favourites.

### 35. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) Raymond C.
Order said "ships in 2 days". It shipped after 9. When it came, the Assam pouch was empty. Not impressed.

### 36. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Esther N.
The Ceylon and the Assam make a great pair for mornings. Only gripe is the pouches don't reseal, so I had to find jars.

### 37. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Ahmed Z.
Liked the Genmaicha a lot, but there was barely a spoonful. Felt like a taster rather than a sampler.

### 38. ★★★★★ (5/5) Molly T.
Arrived fast, every tea was fresh, and the little booklet about where each tea comes from was a nice touch.

### 39. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Ian W.
The temperature guide is in Fahrenheit only. I don't have a thermometer anyway. Just tell me "let it cool for a minute".

### 40. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Rania S.
The Earl Grey was fragrant and the peppermint was really fresh. Box was a bit dented but the teas were fine inside.
