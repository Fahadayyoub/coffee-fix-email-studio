# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (set 5 of 6)

Practice data. These reviews are made up. None of them came from real customers.

---

### 81. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Khalid R.
Strong Assam, delicate White Peony, smoky Lapsang. Good spread of styles. The pouches are just too small.

### 82. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Eleanor B.
Pleasant teas but the brewing card assumes you own a scale. I don't. "3g per 250ml" means nothing to me in teaspoons.

### 83. ★★★★★ (5/5) Mei L.
The Tie Guan Yin tastes like the oolong my grandmother used to make. I nearly cried. Thank you.

### 84. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Gordon T.
Ordered on a Monday, arrived the Monday after next. The cardboard sleeve was torn and one pouch was open.

### 85. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Sana K.
The Genmaicha and the Sencha are both lovely. Took a while to arrive, but the teas were fresh when they did.

### 86. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Alex P.
Okay box. The Earl Grey was the best of it. The peppermint was nice. The rest didn't stand out.

### 87. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) Brenda W.
Half the pouches were unlabelled. I had no idea which was the Dragonwell and which was the Sencha. Brewed them all wrong.

### 88. ★★★★★ (5/5) Rafael D.
Bought it on a whim and it's become a weekend ritual. The Darjeeling with a slice of toast on a Sunday morning. Lovely.

### 89. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Heba M.
The masala chai and the vanilla rooibos were both great with milk. Packaging is a bit over the top, but the teas are good.

### 90. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Steven J.
The box says 12 teas. I counted 10. Two duplicates, two missing. Not the end of the world but sloppy.

### 91. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Laura C.
I loved the jasmine pearls, but there were maybe eight of them. I'd pay a bit more for a proper amount.

### 92. ★★★★★ (5/5) Idris O.
Fast delivery, neat box, fresh leaves. The Hojicha is the one I'll be buying again. Easy five stars.

### 93. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Michelle A.
The brewing guide is a QR code only. My phone wouldn't scan it. A simple printed card would be much easier.

### 94. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Faisal N.
The Ceylon is bright and clean, great with lemon. I'd give five stars if shipping hadn't taken nine days.

### 95. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Rosie G.
Nice gift but the box arrived with a big dent. My friend still liked it. The chamomile was her favourite.

### 96. ★★★★★ (5/5) Tomás V.
I've had a lot of Lapsang and this one is excellent. Smoky but smooth. The White Peony was a nice contrast.

### 97. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) Angela S.
Every tea came in its own plastic sachet. I'd happily trade the fancy packaging for paper pouches and more tea.

### 98. ★★★★☆ (4/5) Zain H.
The oolong was the highlight. Rolled leaves that open up as they brew. Only enough for two sessions though.

### 99. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) Fiona D.
The Dragonwell instructions said boiling water. Pretty sure that's wrong for green tea. Tasted burnt. The rest was fine.

### 100. ★★★★★ (5/5) Kemal B.
Lovely selection, and every tea had clear notes on taste and brewing. The Genmaicha is my new afternoon cup.
