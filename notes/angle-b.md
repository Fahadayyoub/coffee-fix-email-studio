# Angle B: discovering new teas

Subject line ideas for email 01 (launch) of The Steep Club. Drafts only. Nothing is scheduled or sent.

1. A new tea to try every month 🍵
2. Find a tea you'll love. The Steep Club is open.
3. Your next favourite tea is waiting

## Notes

- All three follow `brand/voice.md`: warm, no "best" claims, no health words, no pushy words, and no more than one emoji.
- Line 1 suggests a different tea each month. Use it only once the fact sheet confirms that. If boxes repeat teas, change it to "Fresh tea to try every month 🍵".
- Lines 2 and 3 fit tea-curious beginners best. They promise discovery, not expertise.
